Upsay: Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Upsay: Better words, every day ("Upsay", "the app") is made by Hajime ("I", "me"). This page explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: Upsay has no account and no sign-up. Your practice progress stays on your phone and is never sent to me. If you choose to watch a video to open a day's phrases, Google's AdMob service shows that video and may collect device information to do so. If you buy the unlock, Google Play handles the payment.

What stays on your phone

The phrases you practiced, your answers, your daily plan, your streak, your onboarding answers (for example where you want to sound better) and your reminder time are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded, not shared with me, and not included in device backups. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

Videos (Google AdMob)

The first 3 daily plans are free. After that you can open a day's plan by watching a short video. The video is shown by Google AdMob, and ad-related code only starts when you reach that choice. To show videos, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it indicates;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • interactions with the video (for example whether it was watched or tapped) and diagnostic data.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by me. Google's practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized, using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in the app under Progress → Settings → Privacy settings. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the phone). If you unlock the app, no videos are shown at all.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Unlock everything" is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; I never see your card or payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the phrases and restore the purchase if you reinstall. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

What I do not do

  • I don't ask for your name, email or any account.
  • I don't run my own analytics or collect crash logs through my own servers.
  • I don't sell personal information.
  • The app doesn't use your microphone, camera, contacts or precise location.

Children

Upsay is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact me and I'll help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts using information in a new way, I'll update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released.
